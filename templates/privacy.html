Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 23, 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create a Spendly account, we collect your name, email address, and password. As you use the app, we collect the expense data you enter, including amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data to provide the core functionality of Spendly: logging your expenses, generating spending summaries, and securing your account. We do not use your data for advertising and we do not sell it to third parties.

3. Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored securely and retained for as long as your account remains active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time, after which your data will be permanently removed.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly may rely on a small number of trusted third-party services, such as hosting and font providers, to operate the app. These providers only receive the minimum information necessary to deliver their service and do not have access to your expense data.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us through the contact details provided on our website.
